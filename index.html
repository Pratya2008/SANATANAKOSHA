Valmiki Ramayana

Composed by Adi Kavi Valmiki, the Ramayana charts the journey of Prince Rama of Ayodhya—the embodiment of Maryada (righteous conduct)—through exile, the abduction of Sita, the alliance with Kishkindha, and the cosmic conflict at Lanka.

7 Kandas: Bala, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Sundara, Yuddha, and Uttara Kanda.
Core Philosophy: Duty to truth, ideal governance (Rama Rajya), and unwavering devotion.
Dvapara Yuga • 100,000 Shlokas

Vyasa Mahabharata

Compiled by Sage Krishna Dvaipayana Vyasa, the Mahabharata examines the intricate moral ambiguities of Dharma Sankata (ethical dilemmas) culminating in the 18-day war at Kurukshetra and the spiritual discourse of the Bhagavad Gita.

18 Parvas: From Adi Parva and Sabha Parva to Bhishma Parva (containing the Gita) and Svargarohana.
Core Philosophy: Selfless action (Nishkama Karma), discernment, and cosmic justice.
